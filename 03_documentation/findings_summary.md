# Key Findings Summary

## Executive Overview

Analysis of 5,595 approved loans worth KES 119 million identified critical risk patterns and growth opportunities in the lending portfolio.

---

## 🔴 Critical Risks Identified

### 1. The 100K Loan Problem
- **Default Rate**: 32.1% (vs 24% average)
- **Volume**: 106 loans
- **Annual Loss Exposure**: KES 900K - 1.2M
- **Severity**: 🔴 CRITICAL

**Root Cause**: Loan size exceeds repayment capacity for typical customer profile

**Recommendation**: Cap maximum at 75K OR require co-signer above 75K

---

### 2. High-Risk Counties
- **Thika**: 26.8% default (373 loans)
- **Eldoret**: 26.5% default (422 loans)
- **Severity**: 🔴 HIGH

**Recommendation**: Tighten lending criteria, add +2% risk premium

---

### 3. Small County + Large Loan = Disaster
- **Malindi 100K**: 50% default
- **Kitale 100K**: 50% default
- **Kisumu 100K**: 40% default
- **Severity**: 🔴 CRITICAL

**Recommendation**: Implement county-specific loan caps

---

## ✅ Growth Opportunities

### 1. Kitale County - Hidden Gem
- **Default Rate**: 19.0% (5 points better than average!)
- **Current Volume**: Only 226 loans (severely underutilized)
- **Best Products**: 20K (8.3% default), 75K (7.7% default)
- **Potential**: 🟢 HUGE

**Recommendation**: Launch "Kitale Growth Initiative" targeting 500+ loans

---

### 2. 75K Loan Sweet Spot
- **Default Rate**: 20.7% (3.3 points better than average)
- **Volume**: 299 loans
- **Performance**: Consistent across most counties
- **Potential**: 🟢 HIGH

**Recommendation**: Make 75K the standard maximum

---

### 3. Nairobi Volume Play
- **Default Rate**: 22.8% (below average)
- **Volume**: 2,194 loans (largest market)
- **10K Loans**: 427 volume at 19.9% default
- **75K Loans**: 114 volume at 19.3% default
- **Potential**: 🟢 VERY HIGH

**Recommendation**: Double down on Nairobi growth

---
